Where every number on screen comes from

All sources are public, documented APIs. Wait times come from government feeds; drive times from a user-selectable routing engine; predictions are computed server-side from feed history. ← Back to the prototype · Engineering plan →

4 · Honest limits

  • CBP waits are officer-reported at ~hourly-or-better cadence — treat them as ±10 min ground truth, which is why the UI leads with predictions and ranges, not raw feed numbers.
  • Southbound (into Mexico) has no official feed; those directions would need crowdsourcing or camera partnerships and are out of v1 scope.
  • Google Routes pricing means ETAs are batched (one matrix call per re-rank, not per port) and cached for 2 min.
