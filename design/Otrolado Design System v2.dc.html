Otrolado — product design system · v2.0

Same opinion, tighter system

v1 set the direction: navy structure, one cobalt, cool ground, no shadows. v2 doesn't redesign any of it. It fixes the contrast of secondary text, cuts the radius scale to four values, locks an icon family, adds the production states the product actually hits, and ships the whole thing as tokens.

Rules

Space — 4pt grid
Screen padding 20px. Card padding 16px, hero 20px. Gaps from 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 only — v1's 7 / 9 / 11 / 13 are gone.
Hit targets
Never below 44px. Buttons 48px (14px vertical padding at body size). Icon buttons 44×44 with a 24px glyph.
Borders & elevation
1px line, or 1.5px for outline buttons and focus. No shadows on anything that sits in the page. Sheets and menus may use one: 0 -8px 28px rgba(22,35,74,.14).

Rules
01
Recorded bars are solid line-strong, not line — they carry data and must be visible on mist.
02
The current reading is the one cobalt bar and carries the NOW marker with a 1.5px rule to the axis.
03
Forecast is outlined, never filled. Prediction must never look like measurement.
04
One axis hairline at the baseline, two gridlines in mist. No y labels — the number above the chart is the scale.
05
Bars 6px top radius, square feet. Tabular hour labels so the row never shifts.
Booth meter

One pill per booth, never a percentage bar. The count is the honest unit — drivers can see it themselves at the gate.

Pharr — Reynosa
26 min drive + 15 min border
41m
Otrolado's real question is "which crossing gets me across fastest?" — and that answer is drive time plus border time, not the CBP queue alone. A closer port with a 60-minute line loses to one 25 minutes away with a 15-minute line, and today's hero can't express that.
01
The 48px number becomes total minutes. The split lives under it at metadata size — two numbers, never three.
02
The proportion bar is the only new component: cobalt-light for drive, white for border. It reads at a glance which half is the problem.
03
List rows sort by total too, so the ranking and the hero always agree.
04
Needs a routing source and a location permission. Until both exist, the hero stays the border number and this section stays a proposal.
Voice stays the same: "34 min total to Hidalgo. Leave now." The number still comes first.
